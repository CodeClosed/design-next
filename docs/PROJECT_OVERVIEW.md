# Project Overview: Industrial Corporate Website Starter Template

## 1. Purpose & Scope

This project is a production-quality corporate website starter template engineered for a **B2B industrial engineering and manufacturing company** specializing in **sugar-processing equipment and heavy industrial process machinery**.

Unlike consumer SaaS landing pages or e-commerce templates, this website is engineered to establish engineering authority, demonstrate manufacturing capacity, showcase technical specifications, and convert commercial visitors into qualified Request for Quotation (RFQ) leads.

- **Deployment**: Explicitly out of scope; the deliverable is a thoroughly tested, locally runnable production codebase.
- **Placeholder & Factuality Policy**: Zero fabricated company history, certifications, customer names, financial/production statistics, or project references are disguised as verified facts. All sample data is centralized and explicitly flagged (`isSampleContent: true` / `[Sample Placeholder — Verify Before Publication]`) so the target organization can replace it before launch.

## 2. Target Audience & B2B Personas

1. **Sugar Mill Owners & Managing Directors**: Evaluating partner longevity, turnkey project execution capacity, and plant reliability.
2. **EPC Consultants & Project Engineers**: Reviewing equipment throughput, heating surface areas, operating pressures, dimensional configurations, and technical documentation.
3. **Procurement & Commercial Managers**: Submitting structured RFQs, attaching technical specification sheets/drawings, and verifying quality inspection workflows.
4. **Chief Engineers & Plant Maintenance Heads**: Evaluating after-sales support, spare parts compatibility, and commissioning track record.

## 3. Core Website Objectives

- **Establish Credibility**: Clear presentation of engineering capabilities, quality assurance workflows, and industrial manufacturing discipline.
- **Technical Equipment Catalogue**: Searchable and filterable equipment catalogue with dynamic detail pages (`[slug]`), tabular specifications, applications, and downloadable specification brochures.
- **Turnkey & Process Context**: Dedicated industry landing pages (Sugar Manufacturing, Processing, Refining, Distillery & Allied) and 7-stage engineering/manufacturing capability breakdown.
- **Actionable Lead Generation**: Real client + server-side validated General Enquiry and Technical RFQ forms with specification file upload, spam protection (honeypot + rate limiting), and modular email provider abstraction with honest development preview feedback.

| `/quality` | Quality management philosophy, inspection stages, material traceability & compliance policy |

| `/contact` | Office/factory details, map placeholder, WhatsApp link, General Enquiry & RFQ forms with file attachment |
| `/privacy` & `/terms` | Legal template pages flagged for legal review |
